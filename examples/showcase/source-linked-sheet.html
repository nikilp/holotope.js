Source-linked R4 sheet

One four-dimensional triangular sheet is the model. It stretches, folds, and rests on a separate finite obstacle, and everything below is a view of it — the two canvases are two lossy maps of the same R4 state, not two simulations. Selecting in either canvas names the source triangle that produced the pixel, and says plainly whether that pixel determines a point in R4 at all. Deformable materials · Incremental potentials · Representation provenance

Discrete cosine-fold bending — not a shell model. The fold stiffness here is an unsigned, mesh-dependent discrete term whose energy is quartic in the fold angle. It does not converge to a continuum bending energy under refinement, and its stiffness is not a transferable material constant. This page is a demonstration of source-retained geometry, mechanics, representation, and selection — not a cloth or shell simulator.
