Interaction through the hidden direction

A box and a ball in four-dimensional space, both free to move, both solid to each other. You see one three-dimensional slice — a knife through 4D — and, if you ask for it, the faint projection of the whole objects. Press Play: the ball is launched once along w, the direction the slice cannot show, and everything after that is integration and contact in the released physics — no path, no script. The question: when something you cannot see in this cut changes the motion of something you can, how do you find the cause? Turn the knife toward w, or show the projection. A guided sequence below walks the question through five short stages; every control it touches says whether it moves the observer or the clock.
