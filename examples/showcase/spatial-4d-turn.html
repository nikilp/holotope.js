Turn through the same object

A block and a small ball in four-dimensional space, seen through a three-dimensional slice — a knife through 4D; the cut surface is what the screen shows. The block never moves. The ball can: press Play and it circles a fixed point in 4D along a stated path — no force, no physics — while the knife stays where you left it. Turn tilts the knife. Offset slides it sideways. Dragging the view only walks around the cut. The two objects never touch or affect each other.

Slides the section along its current normal. This is not a second turn. Marks on each slider are for the other slider’s current value.

The ball circles a fixed point in 4D, in the z–w plane. How much of that circle lies within the knife’s reach depends on where the knife is: about 57% at the reference, none at a quarter turn with no offset. Marks are for the current turn and offset.
